# Paid pilot offer — proposal, not published or approved

## Product copy

**Keep contractor estimates moving toward a clear next step.**

QuoteTend organizes estimates, shows which follow-ups are due, prepares editable drafts, and keeps a record of contacts and outcomes. Review Follow-up Results to see recorded activity and estimate value won after reported follow-ups.

You contact customers through your existing phone or email tools. QuoteTend does not send messages automatically, confirm delivery, collect your customers' payments, or prove that it caused a sale. Won estimate value is not collected revenue.

Private business account, one owner login, CSV import/export, editable drafts, scheduled follow-up lists and conservative results reporting. Existing estimate IDs are protected during import. Shared-email opt-outs stop follow-up prompts for matching records. Supported pilot capacity: up to 5,000 estimates subject to the 4.8 MB saved-workspace limit. USD and one Oregon (Pacific Time) business calendar. Browser demo data is separate.

*Corrected in Sprint 4A: this line previously said "the service's Central Time calendar". Since Sprint 3A the product uses one America/Los_Angeles business day for every account, whatever the server's time zone.*

Annual pricing, lifetime purchases and extra tiers should wait for real retention/support evidence. This price is a testable proposal, not validated willingness to pay.

## Cancellation, refund and support proposals

- Monthly recurring charge, clearly disclosed before purchase. Cancel future renewals through the hosted billing portal or verified support request. Proposed access continues until the paid-through date; an operator applies that date manually.
- Proposed first-payment refund on request within 14 calendar days. Later refunds assessed for duplicate charges or service failure, subject to applicable obligations. No guarantee of provider processing times. These are business proposals, not legal conclusions or a deployed refund feature.
- Proposed support contact: **quotetend@gmail.com**. Proposed response target: one business day, Monday–Friday, Central Time; no 24/7 coverage or uptime SLA promised. Confirm this is sustainable before publishing.
- Offer export assistance before account suspension. Do not promise automatic deletion or a fixed retention window until active records, logs, recovery copies and off-service backup handling have an approved operational procedure.
- Never request customers' passwords, recovery codes or card details in support messages. Redact customer information from troubleshooting examples.

### The offer, as delivered today

| Dimension | Honest assessment |
|---|---|
| Value delivered | A daily "who to call back" list with values, two-tap call logging that always sets a next date, opt-out safety, conservative results. No sending, no integration, no owner reminders. |
| Setup burden | Export to CSV, add an estimate-number column if missing, match columns, review opt-outs, choose a first follow-up date. Roughly a 30–60 minute assisted session (estimate, not measured with a contractor). |
| Training | About 15 minutes for the daily loop. Import and corrections need the founder at first. |
| Monthly owner effort | A few minutes per working day to open the list and log calls; re-import or add new estimates weekly. No sync. |
| Expected support | Imports and mappings, forgotten passwords (no email reset; recovery code or administrator), "why isn't this in Results?", backups. |
| Missing capabilities | Import from their estimating tool, owner reminders, team logins, phone-first logging, sending. |
| Switching cost | Low to start (CSV in, CSV out). The real cost is the ongoing second list. |

### What a contractor must believe before paying

1. Estimates go quiet without a planned callback, and that has cost them work.
2. They will open a second list most working days.
3. Getting estimates in is a one-time job, or a short weekly one, not double entry of everything.
4. Their customers' details are safe with QuoteTend (requires the hosting, backup and privacy gates).
5. The subscription costs less than the value of the work it helps them keep. QuoteTend cannot claim it causes sales; the owner has to judge this.

- Success criteria agreed and written **before** starting, for example: the owner opens QuoteTend on most working days, logs calls without prompting, and at the end says whether they would pay to continue. ### Evidence ladder (weakest to strongest)

| Level | Evidence | Counts as demand? |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Compliments, "sounds useful", "send me a link" | No |
| 1 | Describes a specific recent slipped estimate and its value | Problem evidence only |
| 2 | Shows their actual estimate list or export screen | Workflow evidence |
| 3 | Agrees to a second working session on a set date | Weak intent |
| 4 | Agrees to a concrete onboarding date | Moderate intent |
| 5 | Accepts written pilot terms, including the price | Strong intent |
| 6 | Pays, or signs a commitment to pay from a start date | Willingness to pay |

Record the highest level reached per business in the [notes template](interviews/INTERVIEW_TRACKER.md). Do not round up.

## Evidence rule

Measured facts: contacts logged, supported estimate lists, recorded outcome values and history coverage.
Customer reports: what they say happened outside QuoteTend.
Interpretation: possible benefits to investigate—not proven incremental revenue.

Use the [baseline](interviews/PILOT_BASELINE.md) and [results review](interviews/RESULTS_REVIEW.md). No testimonials, customer findings or revenue figures have been invented.
