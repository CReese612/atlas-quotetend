Check these before sharing any result:

- Use the drill-down lists to verify the included estimates; do not count the same estimate value once per contact.
- Keep the selected cohort, current outcome observation date and baseline period explicit. Outcomes may happen after the cohort end date.
- Review backdated contacts, corrected amounts/outcome dates, reopened estimates, missing logs and restores. A restore starts a new measurement period, not evidence of no earlier activity.
- Identify differences in lead mix, seasonality, staffing, pricing and other tools. Do not compare unlike populations as a controlled experiment.
- Use “estimate value” unless independently verified payment data supports another term. No QuoteTend contact is provider-verified in this version.
- Record criticism and continued-use behavior. Positive comments alone are not proof of value or willingness to pay.
