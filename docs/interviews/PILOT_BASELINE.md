Ask about the last actual estimates before showing the demo:

1. Where are estimates created and tracked? Who owns follow-up? Which existing reminders or automations are used?
2. For the baseline period, how many estimates were sent, and what was their total value? What source supports those numbers? Are taxes, discounts and revisions included consistently?
3. How many were won, lost or still open? Does “won” mean customer acceptance, scheduled work, a deposit or paid-in-full? Keep those definitions separate.
4. How many received a follow-up, through which channels, and after how long? Can the business verify this or is it an estimate from memory?
5. What work does follow-up involve each week? What is forgotten or duplicated? Record examples and contrary evidence, not just complaints.
6. During the pilot, will job size, lead sources, seasonality, staffing, pricing or other marketing change? What could explain different outcomes besides QuoteTend?

Separate **source-backed facts**, **participant recollection**, **direct observations**, and **our hypotheses**. A before/after comparison can describe a change; it does not by itself establish causation. Historical data that cannot be verified should remain unknown, not zero.
