Features

Everything that happens after the estimate goes out.

QuoteTend picks up where your estimating tool stops: what you’ve sent, what’s due, what was said and how it ended. Here’s what’s built today, and what isn’t yet.

  • Overview dashboard
  • Estimate tracking
  • Search & filters
  • Customer & project details
  • Follow-up queues
  • Message drafts
  • Activity recording
  • Outcome tracking
  • Follow-up Results
  • CSV import & export
  • Available today & planned

Screens on this page use the fictional Fernbrook Roofing & Exteriors demo account and show only what the customer app does today. The demo itself also previews a few planned conveniences, such as trade tags, street and phone details, and some extra filters and charts.

Your pipeline

See every open estimate — and what it’s worth.

01Overview dashboard

The day’s follow-up work at a glance.

Start the day with your open pipeline value, the follow-ups that are due or overdue, and the wins you’ve recorded, with the estimates that need attention listed first.

  • Open pipeline: sent estimates still awaiting a decision
  • Due and overdue follow-ups, oldest promise first
  • Recent activity across your estimates

02Estimate tracking & management

Every estimate, from sent to decided.

Add estimates by hand or by import, edit them as details change, and move each one through draft, sent, won or lost.

Search and filtering

  • Search across customers, jobs, estimate IDs and emails
  • Filter by draft, sent, won, lost or do-not-contact
  • Open pipeline and won totals calculated from your records

03Customer & project details

What you need to know before you call.

Each estimate keeps the customer’s name and email, the job, the amount and estimate date, its status and next follow-up date, your notes, a saved draft and its full activity history.

Phone numbers, street addresses and trade tags appear in the demo as previews of planned customer-app fields.

Follow-up

Know who to call, what to say and what happened.

04Follow-up queues

Know exactly who to call today.

Overdue, due today, upcoming and estimates that still need a date — sorted so the oldest promise rises to the top. Won, lost, draft and opted-out estimates stay out of the way.

  • Due & overdue, Upcoming and Needs a date queues
  • Days overdue and estimate value at a glance
  • Customers who opt out leave every queue automatically

05Editable follow-up drafts

Start every follow-up with the right words.

Each estimate comes with a follow-up draft built from its details. Personalize it, save it, and copy it into your own email or texting app.

  • Drafts include the customer, job and estimate amount
  • Edit freely and save your version with the estimate
  • Copy in one click and send from the tools you already use

QuoteTend never sends messages on its own. Copying a draft doesn’t send anything or record a contact — you stay in control.

06Activity & outcomes

A clear record of every conversation and decision.

Log the call, text or email that happened, set the next check-in, and record won or lost when the homeowner decides. The activity history stays with the estimate.

Manual activity recording

  • Contact date, method, note and next follow-up in one step
  • Only contacts you log are recorded — nothing is added for you

Outcome tracking

  • Won and lost outcomes clear the follow-up and keep the history
  • Do-not-contact requests apply to every estimate with that email

Results & your data

Honest reporting, and data you can take with you.

07Follow-up Results

See what your follow-up activity adds up to.

Follow-up Results shows contacts logged, estimates followed up and the outcomes recorded afterward — and every number opens the estimates behind it.

Reports reflect what your team records. Won values are estimate amounts, not collected revenue or proof of what caused a sale.

08CSV import & export

Start with the spreadsheet you already have.

Export your open estimates as CSV. QuoteTend matches your columns, converts dates and dollar amounts, and previews every row before anything is saved. Existing estimates are never overwritten.

Export your estimates as CSV whenever you like, or download a workspace backup that keeps saved drafts and activity history.

Straight answers

What’s available today — and what isn’t yet.

QuoteTend is getting ready for early access. We’d rather you know exactly what you’re getting.

Available today Built

The core estimate follow-up workflow, end to end.

  • Estimate tracking with search and status filters
  • Follow-up queues: due & overdue, upcoming, needs a date
  • Editable follow-up drafts you copy and send yourself
  • Contact logging with next follow-up dates
  • Won, lost and do-not-contact outcomes with activity history
  • CSV import with column matching and row-by-row preview
  • Follow-up Results reporting on recorded activity
  • CSV export and workspace backups

On the roadmap Not available yet

Where we’re headed, shaped with early customers. None of these exist in QuoteTend today.

  • Automated email and text follow-up
  • Integrations with estimating tools, CRMs and calendars
  • Team accounts with assigned follow-ups
  • Sales intelligence and advanced pipeline analytics

Good to know today

  • Each business has one owner login.
  • Estimates come in by CSV export or manual entry.
  • Amounts are in US dollars.
