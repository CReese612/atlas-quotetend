How it works

From a pile of estimates to a clear next step.

Five steps your team already understands. QuoteTend keeps them in order and keeps the record, while you stay in charge of every conversation.

Start the guided tour Request Early Access

The workflow

Five steps, one record per estimate.

  1. 1

    Step 1

    Import or add your open estimates

    You
    Export open estimates as a CSV file from your spreadsheet or estimating tool, or add them one at a time.
    QuoteTend
    Matches your columns, converts dates and dollar amounts, and previews every row before anything is saved. Existing estimates are never overwritten.
  2. 2

    Step 2

    Review the pipeline and see what needs attention

    You
    Open your follow-ups at the start of the day.
    QuoteTend
    Sorts sent estimates into due & overdue, upcoming and needs a date, oldest promise first. Drafts, closed and opted-out estimates stay out of the way.
  3. 3

    Step 3

    Prepare a personalized follow-up draft

    You
    Open the estimate, make the draft sound like you, and copy it.
    QuoteTend
    Starts the draft from the estimate’s customer, job and amount, and saves your edited version with the estimate.
  4. 4

    Step 4

    Contact the customer, then record it

    You
    Call, text or email from your own phone or inbox. Then log the contact in QuoteTend.
    QuoteTend
    Saves the contact date, method, note and next follow-up date to the estimate’s activity history.
  5. 5

    Step 5

    Record the outcome and review results

    You
    Mark the estimate won or lost when the homeowner decides.
    QuoteTend
    Clears the follow-up, keeps the history, and updates Follow-up Results with the contacts and outcomes your team recorded.

Straight answers

What QuoteTend does — and what stays with you.

  • A draft isn’t a sent message.

    Editing, saving or copying a draft doesn’t send anything and doesn’t record a contact. QuoteTend never sends messages on its own.

  • You contact customers yourself.

    Calls, texts and emails happen in the tools you already use. Log a contact only after it actually happened.

  • A recorded win isn’t proof of cause.

    Follow-up Results reflects what your team records. Won values are estimate amounts, not collected revenue, and a win after a follow-up doesn’t show the follow-up caused the sale.
