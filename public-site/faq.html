The product

What does QuoteTend do?

QuoteTend keeps track of estimates after they’re sent. It lists every outstanding estimate, shows which customers are due for a follow-up, prepares an editable message draft, and keeps a record of each contact and outcome your team logs. See how it works.

Who is QuoteTend designed for?

Roofing, siding, window and door, gutter and exterior remodeling contractors who sell with written estimates and follow up by phone, text or email. It fits owner-led businesses that track estimates in a spreadsheet, an estimating tool, an inbox or a notebook today. Each business currently has one owner login.

Does QuoteTend send messages automatically?

NoQuoteTend prepares an editable draft for each follow-up. You copy it into your own email or texting app, send it yourself, then log the contact in QuoteTend.

Automated sending is on the roadmap. It isn’t available today, and we won’t describe it as available until it is.

What does Follow-up Results actually measure?

Only what your team records in QuoteTend: contacts logged, estimates followed up, and the won or lost outcomes recorded afterward. Every number opens the estimates behind it.

Values are estimate amounts. They are not collected revenue, and a win recorded after a follow-up doesn’t prove the follow-up caused the sale. QuoteTend doesn’t claim credit for any sale.

Can I try a demo?

YesThe interactive demo is open to everyone with no sign-up. It uses a fictional roofing and exteriors company, runs entirely in your browser, and can be reset at any time. A five-step guided tour shows the core workflow.

Your data and current tools

Do I need to replace my existing CRM?

NoQuoteTend does one job: keeping sent estimates moving toward a decision. It works alongside the estimating software, CRM or spreadsheet you already use.

It doesn’t connect to other systems today. You bring estimates in with a CSV export or by adding them directly. Integrations are on the roadmap, not available yet.

Can I upload estimates from a spreadsheet?

YesExport your estimates as a CSV file (save Excel workbooks as CSV first). QuoteTend matches your column names, converts common date and dollar formats, and previews every row before anything is saved.

Estimates already in QuoteTend are never overwritten, conflicting duplicates are held for review, and rows with missing information are flagged so you can fix the source file.

How are customer opt-outs handled?

Mark a customer do-not-contact and their estimates leave every follow-up queue. The request applies to every estimate with the same customer email, it’s noted in the activity history, and removing an opt-out asks you to confirm first. If an imported row carrying an opt-out has a problem, QuoteTend blocks the import until the row is fixed, so the preference isn’t lost.

Because you contact customers yourself, honoring an opt-out outside QuoteTend is still up to your team.

Early access

Is QuoteTend available now?

Early accessNot generally, yet. We’re preparing assisted early access for a small group of exterior contractors. Timing, fit and early-customer pricing are discussed directly, before anyone commits to anything.

How does early access work?

Send a request through the early-access form. Setup is assisted: we’ll start with a conversation about how your team handles estimates today. Then we’ll help you export your open estimates, match the columns, and set follow-up dates, so your queue reflects real work from the start.

Before you import any real customer information, we’ll walk through where your data is stored and how it’s backed up.

How can I contact QuoteTend?

The early-access form is the best way to reach us. Add your question in “Anything we should know?” and we’ll reply to the email address you give us.
