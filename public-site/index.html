What QuoteTend does

One clear place for every estimate after it leaves your office.

  • 01Organize

    Every outstanding estimate, in one list.

    Customer, job, amount and status for each estimate you’ve sent, with your open pipeline totaled automatically.

    Estimate tracking

  • 02Prioritize

    Know who needs a call today.

    Follow-ups sort into overdue, due today, upcoming and needs a date, so the oldest promise is always first.

    Follow-up queues

  • 03Track

    A record of every follow-up and decision.

    Log each call, text or email after it happens, set the next check-in, and record won or lost when the homeowner decides.

    Activity & outcomes

Nothing is sent automatically. You reach customers from your own phone or email, then record what happened.
