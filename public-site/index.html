The product

One clear place for every estimate after it leaves your office.

QuoteTend picks up where your estimating tool stops: what you’ve sent, what’s due, what was said and how it ended.

01Estimate visibility

See every open estimate — and what it’s worth.

Find any estimate by customer, job, estimate ID or email, filter by status, and see the open pipeline value at a glance.

  • Search across customers, jobs, estimate IDs and emails
  • Filter by draft, sent, won, lost or do-not-contact
  • Open pipeline and won totals calculated from your records

02Overdue & upcoming follow-ups

Know exactly who to call today.

Overdue, due today, upcoming and estimates that still need a date — sorted so the oldest promise rises to the top. Won, lost, draft and opted-out estimates stay out of the way.

  • Due & overdue, Upcoming and Needs a date queues
  • Days overdue and estimate value at a glance
  • Customers who opt out leave every queue automatically

03Editable follow-up drafts

Start every follow-up with the right words.

Each estimate comes with a follow-up draft built from its details. Personalize it, save it, and copy it into your own email or texting app.

  • Drafts include the customer, job and estimate amount
  • Edit freely and save your version with the estimate
  • Copy in one click and send from the tools you already use

QuoteTend never sends messages on its own. Copying a draft doesn’t send anything or record a contact — you stay in control.

04Contact & outcome tracking

A clear record of every conversation and decision.

Log the call, text or email that happened, set the next check-in, and record won or lost when the homeowner decides. The activity history stays with the estimate.

  • Contact date, method, note and next follow-up in one step
  • Won and lost outcomes clear the follow-up and keep the history
  • Do-not-contact requests apply to every estimate with that email

05Reporting & pipeline

See what your follow-up activity adds up to.

Follow-up Results shows contacts logged, estimates followed up and the outcomes recorded afterward — and every number opens the estimates behind it.

Reports reflect what your team records. Won values are estimate amounts, not collected revenue or proof of what caused a sale.

06CSV onboarding

Start with the spreadsheet you already have.

Export your open estimates as CSV. QuoteTend matches your columns, converts dates and dollar amounts, and previews every row before anything is saved. Existing estimates are never overwritten.

FAQ

Practical questions, straight answers.

The fastest way to judge fit is to click through the demo with your own process in mind.

Does QuoteTend replace my CRM?

NoQuoteTend does one job: keeping sent estimates moving toward a decision. It works alongside the estimating software, CRM or spreadsheet you already use.

It doesn’t connect to other systems today. You bring estimates in with a CSV export or by adding them directly. Integrations are on the roadmap, not available yet.

Can I import my existing estimates?

YesExport your estimates as a CSV file (save Excel workbooks as CSV first). QuoteTend matches your column names, converts common date and dollar formats, and previews every row before anything is saved.

Estimates already in QuoteTend are never overwritten, conflicting duplicates are held for review, and rows with missing information are flagged so you can fix the source file.

Does QuoteTend send messages automatically?

NoQuoteTend prepares an editable draft for each follow-up. You copy it into your own email or texting app, send it yourself, then log the contact in QuoteTend.

Automated sending is on the roadmap. It isn’t available today, and we won’t describe it as available until it is.

Can I try a demo?

YesThe interactive demo is open to everyone with no sign-up. It uses a fictional roofing and exteriors company, runs entirely in your browser, and can be reset at any time. A five-step guided tour shows the core workflow.

How will onboarding work?

Early-access setup will be assisted. We’ll start with a conversation about how your team handles estimates today. Then we’ll help you export your open estimates, match the columns, and set follow-up dates, so your queue reflects real work from the start.

Before you import any real customer information, we’ll walk through where your data is stored and how it’s backed up.

Is QuoteTend available for early customers?

Early accessWe’re preparing assisted early access for a small group of exterior contractors. Timing, fit and early-customer pricing are discussed directly, before anyone commits to anything.

Where do the reporting numbers come from?

Only from what your team records in QuoteTend: contacts logged, follow-up dates and won or lost outcomes. Values are estimate amounts. They are not collected revenue, and QuoteTend doesn’t claim credit for any sale.
