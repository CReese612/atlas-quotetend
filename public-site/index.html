FAQ

A few practical questions.

Does QuoteTend send follow-ups automatically?

No. QuoteTend provides editable drafts and a place to record contact activity that happened outside the product.

Is the interactive demo real customer data?

No. Every company, person, estimate, activity, and outcome in the demo is fictional and stored only in your browser.

Can I reset the demo?

Yes. Use the Reset demo control in the demo header to restore its original fictional workspace.

Does QuoteTend replace a CRM?

This product preview focuses on estimate follow-up. It does not claim CRM integrations or replace every customer-management workflow.
